[mindfork 0.28.1](https://github.com/vshylov/mindfork-rs/releases) is out. It is a
small release: three fixes, all in the Gemini mode, which also reaches Gemma 4 through
the same API key.

## Thinking, asked in the terms each model takes

The Gemini API has two ways to say how much a model should think. Gemini 2.5 takes a
budget of tokens; Gemini 3 and later take a level, such as `low` or `high`. The app
told the two apart by looking for `gemini-3` in the model's name. So every newer name
took the 2.5 branch: the Gemini 4 models, the `gemini-*-latest` aliases, and Gemma 4.

What that did depended on the model:

- Gemma 4 refuses a budget. On Gemma 4 31B and 26B, every turn with thoughts on ended
  in an error.
- The Gemini 4 models accept a budget, loosely. Asked not to think at all,
  `gemini-4-neon` still thought for 181 tokens. That lands on the turns meant to be
  quick, such as the one that names the chat.

Now every model after 2.5 is asked in levels, and 2.5 keeps its budget. Not every model
has every level: Gemini 4 has no `minimal`, and Gemma 4 takes `minimal` and `high` but
not `low`. So a refused level is asked once more. When the refusal names the levels the
model has, the next one up is asked. When it names none, no level is sent, and the
model thinks at its own depth. The answer is remembered, so later turns get it right
the first time.

This was checked on five models: `gemini-4-neon`, `gemini-4-hydrogen`,
`gemini-3.8-flash`, `gemma-4-31b-it` and `gemini-2.5-flash`. Thoughts on, a high and a
low effort, and a turn with thinking muted were each answered on every one.

## Gemini gets its replies back with their signatures

Gemini signs the reasoning behind each reply with an opaque token, sent on the reply's
last part. Google recommends sending it back with the rest of the conversation. The API
does not insist, but it warns that the reasoning can suffer without it.

The app already kept the signature of a reply that called a tool. A reply of plain text
often carries its signature on an empty last part, and that part was dropped, signature
and all. The signature is now kept in the chat beside the reply and sent back with every
later turn, also after a switch to another Gemini model mid-conversation, as Google's
guidance asks. It is never shown.

## A reply Gemini throws away is told

Gemini can think a reply through and then discard it as malformed. It ends with
`MALFORMED_RESPONSE` and sends no text, and the app took that for an ordinary end. In
the conversation where this was found, a turn showed more than 200 lines of thoughts
and then stopped, with no answer and no word of why. After a restart, only part of
those thoughts was left.

Now the feed says the reply was cut short: Gemini discarded the reply it generated as
malformed. It names `Ctrl+R` to generate the reply again. The thoughts stay in the chat,
so a restart shows what the turn did.

## Your data

One field is added to a chat file: the signature of a Gemini reply. Nothing is migrated:
the settings stay at schema 7 and the chats at 9. Going back to 0.28.0 needs no backup
restored. This was checked with the published 0.28.0, on a Gemini conversation this
version wrote. It opens the chat with every message, and the first time it saves the
chat, it leaves the signature out.
