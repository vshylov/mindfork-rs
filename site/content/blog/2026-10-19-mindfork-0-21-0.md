[mindfork 0.21.0](https://github.com/vshylov/mindfork-rs/releases) is out. DeepSeek
has a mode of its own, beside OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Grok and OpenRouter, and the
token counter has moved out of the status line.

## Why a mode, when `external` already worked

0.20.0 made DeepSeek's own API work in the `external` mode, the one that points at
any OpenAI-compatible URL. Measuring what a mode of its own would add turned up two
defects in that route:

- **`/continue` failed, or doubled a reply.** DeepSeek does not continue a cut
  reply. With thinking on, the default, it refused the request with an error; with
  thinking off it answered with a new reply from the beginning, and the app glued it
  onto the part already written.
- **`deepseek-v4-pro` dropped images without a word.** It takes a request with an
  image, says it sees none, and asked what colour a red square is, it answers
  "brown". DeepSeek's model list says the model takes text only, in a key the
  `external` route does not read.

## What the `deepseek` mode knows

Pick `deepseek` under *Model/server → Mode*, paste a key from
`platform.deepseek.com`, and choose `deepseek-flash` or `deepseek-v4-pro` from the
list DeepSeek publishes. The list shows each model's context window.

- **The window**, 1 048 576 tokens, so history compression knows when to start.
- **Which model takes images.** On `deepseek-v4-pro` an image is kept back, and the
  app says so, as it does for any engine without vision.
- **DeepSeek's own thinking switch**, sent in its spelling from the first request.
  Titles, history compression and impersonation do not reason there.
- **Only the sampling settings DeepSeek reads**: temperature, top-p, the two
  penalties, the seed and the reply length. Fields such as top-k or min-p, which it
  drops without a word, are not offered.
- **`/continue`**, through DeepSeek's prefix completion. It resumes a cut story
  mid-sentence, measured on both models. DeepSeek continues only a conversation
  without tool calls. In one that has them, `/continue` says so, and `/regen`
  writes the reply again.

DeepSeek has no embeddings, so a knowledge base under this mode needs another
embedder, as under Claude or Grok. The `external` route at
`https://api.deepseek.com` still works. `/continue` is refused there now, with a note
that points to the mode.

## The token counter, on the conversation's frame

`tokens: 14587 (reasoning 10 · cache 91%)` was the widest item in the status line and
pushed key hints out of it for the length of every turn. It now sits at the bottom
right corner of the conversation's frame. In a window too narrow for it the notes go
first, then the counter, never a cut number. Under eight rows, where the conversation
has no frame, it stays in the status line. *Settings → Interface* can turn either
note off.

## Your data

`settings.json` moves from schema 6 to 7, and the chat files from 5 to 6. Only the
version changes, so that 0.20.0 refuses settings or a chat that name the new mode as
a newer version's data. Without the step it would refuse the settings as a broken file
and leave such a chat out of its list without a word. Both were measured on the
0.20.0 binary. As with every schema change, the files are backed up before they are
stamped. Notes and backups do not change.
