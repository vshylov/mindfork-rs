[mindfork 0.27.0](https://github.com/vshylov/mindfork-rs/releases) is out. The app now
takes the mouse itself, on every screen, so the wheel, clicks and drags do what they
look like they do. Before, it took the mouse only in a mode you switched on with
`Ctrl+W`, and only in the chat. There are three smaller additions: folding one block
of a conversation, copying part of one, and copying from every text field.

## The app holds the mouse

The *Mouse* setting (Settings → Interface → Behavior) is `app` by default.

**`app`.** The app answers the mouse. Your terminal's own selection is still a
modifier away: `Shift` in most terminals, `Option` in iTerm2, `Fn` in Terminal.app.

**`terminal`.** The mouse stays with the terminal. Use it for a terminal with no such
modifier, such as VS Code on macOS, or with a screen reader. The legacy terminal mode
also keeps the mouse with the terminal.

There is no mode to toggle any more, so `Ctrl+W`, `/mouse` and the `mouse:` light on
the status bar are gone. `Ctrl+W` was also the key a browser tab closes the tab on.

## Selecting in the conversation

**How to select.** A drag selects text, a double click takes a word, and a triple click
takes the line. Hold the drag above or below the conversation and it scrolls.

**What `Ctrl+C` copies is what you see**:
- not the coloured bar on the left or the padding;
- a wrapped paragraph as one line;
- formatted text without its markdown;
- a quote without its `>`, and a tool card or the thinking without the lines along
  their edge.

`F5` still copies a conversation's source.

**The message box** takes the same double and triple click, and a drag past its edge
scrolls a long draft.

**While the assistant answers**, nothing in the conversation can be selected: the text
is still moving.

## Every other screen

**The wheel and clicks.** The chat list, settings, the self-model, search results, the
tasks, the notes, the changes and the help all take the mouse.
- The wheel scrolls what is under the pointer. A selected row stays where it is until
  the list's edge pushes it along, so the arrow keys continue from where you see it.
- A click selects a row or opens a tab, and a double click does what `Enter` does.
- The popups over the chat take clicks too: the link, emoji, spelling and profile
  pickers, and the conversation's search bar.

**Text you only read** can be selected and copied: the help, an opened note, a diff, a
version of the self-model.

**`Ctrl+C` and `Ctrl+X` now work in every text field**, where a selection could be made
but not copied before. That covers renaming a chat, the settings' editors, search and
model filter, the notes' search, and others.

**Where a copy is reported.** A copy says how it went on the status bar for a few
seconds: copied, copied through your terminal (OSC 52) over SSH, too big, or failed.
It used to be a line in the conversation.

## One block at a time

`Ctrl+T` and `Ctrl+O` fold or unfold all the thinking and all the tool calls at once.

Now a click on a block's `▸` or `▾` pill folds or unfolds just that block: the thinking,
one tool call, or the summary of earlier messages. The chat remembers it.

To make that possible, an expanded tool call now ends its header on a `▾ details` pill.
`Ctrl+T` and `Ctrl+O` still bring every block of their kind to one state, and forget
the clicks.

## Copying part of a conversation

`/copy last 3` copies the last three messages, and `/copy first 2` the first two. Without
a number it copies one.
- Messages are counted as the conversation shows them: a reply built over several rounds
  of tool calls is one. That is the number the chat list shows beside the chat.
- The format is the whole copy's, without the chat's title.
- The status bar says how many messages were copied.

Every copy of a conversation (`F5`, `/copy` and a Markdown `/export`) now labels each
reply once. A reply with several rounds of tool calls used to copy as two or three
`Assistant:` blocks.

## Fixes

**No freeze while the mouse moves.** The screen no longer stalls while the mouse moves.
Over SSH or in a long chat, two mouse reports arriving together were taken for the start
of a paste. A wheel spin then drew nothing until the mouse stopped.

**No jump when a chip appears.** The chat no longer jumps a row when the status bar
shows a chip such as "copied". Where the key hints just fitted on one row, the chip
moved the whole screen up and back down a few seconds later.

## Your data

Nothing is migrated: the settings stay at schema 7 and the chats at 8. Two fields are
new, the *Mouse* setting and a chat's folded blocks.

Going back to 0.26.4 needs no backup restored. This was checked with 0.26.4 on data this
version wrote:
- 0.26.4 starts and opens the chat.
- It drops the *Mouse* setting at its first save, and this version then reads it as
  `app`.
- It forgets the folded blocks when it next saves that chat.
