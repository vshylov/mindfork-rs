[mindfork 0.26.3](https://github.com/vshylov/mindfork-rs/releases) is out. It is a
small release: a key for pasting a picture, and two fixes.

## `Alt+V` pastes the clipboard's picture

Take a screenshot, or copy a picture in an editor, press `Ctrl+V` in the input box —
and in Windows Terminal nothing happened. The terminal keeps `Ctrl+V` for its own text
paste. A picture has no text, so the terminal sent the app nothing, and only
`/image paste` could reach the clipboard.

`Alt+V` does what `/image paste` does, with a key. It stages the clipboard's picture
for your next message — or the picture files you copied in a file manager, under
their own names — and leaves what you were typing as it was. It works in every
terminal and under any keyboard layout. With no picture on the clipboard it pastes no
text: it says there is no picture, and names `/image attach` for one on disk or at an
address. `Ctrl+V` still pastes a picture where the terminal passes the key on, and
text everywhere.

The help lists both keys.

## The token counter on Gemini

The counter on the feed's bottom border adds up the conversation and the reply, and
notes the reasoning in it. On Gemini, a turn that thought went wrong there:

- a build from source crashed as soon as the model's thoughts arrived in more than one
  piece;
- a release build showed a reply count that was off;
- the reasoning note was several times the real figure.

Gemini reports its counts with every piece of the reply, each one a running total,
and the counter took each report as a correction to apply again. Now each report
replaces the last. The figure climbs with the reply, counts the thoughts as part of
it, as on every other provider, and ends on Gemini's own numbers.

## Also

The help's line for `/profile system` now says which conversations take the edit:
the new ones and those not yet begun. It also names `/system` for a conversation
already under way.

## Your data

Nothing is migrated: the settings stay at schema 7 and the chats at 8. Going back to
0.26.2 needs no backup restored. This was checked with 0.26.2 on data this version
wrote: a Gemini conversation with a picture pasted by `Alt+V`.
