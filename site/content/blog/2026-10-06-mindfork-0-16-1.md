[mindfork 0.16.1](https://github.com/vshylov/mindfork-rs/releases) is out. It
finishes what [0.16.0](@/blog/2026-10-05-mindfork-0-16-0.md) started for
[Ollama](https://ollama.com): that release read Ollama's context window, so a
long conversation is folded into a summary before Ollama would cut it. This one
is about the cut that happens anyway.

## Two cuts, and one that matters

A server that cannot hold a prompt should refuse it, and llama-server does.
Ollama answers, and cuts. Measured on Ollama 0.35.1, it cuts in one of two ways:

- **The oldest messages go.** A conversation over the window loses its first
  messages whole until the rest fits. The instructions and your last message
  stay, and what Ollama reports is just under the window — which is where
  mindfork folds the conversation anyway.
- **The start of the prompt goes.** When the instructions and your last message
  alone do not fit — a long paste, a large tool result — the prompt is cut to
  half the window, from the start. The instructions go first. In the test, a
  model asked for a code word from its instructions answered with fragments of
  the pasted text instead.

Either way the reply arrives as if nothing happened, and what Ollama reports is
the cut size. Before this release, mindfork took that size at its word.

## What mindfork does now

It knows the exact size of the chat's previous request, and a request that
extends it cannot hold less. When Ollama reports less, the request was cut:

- **The feed says so, once a session**: how much of the request the server
  processed, and how to give Ollama a larger window — `OLLAMA_CONTEXT_LENGTH`,
  or the context slider in its app's settings.
- **The conversation is folded into a summary** when there is anything to fold,
  even before Ollama has said what its window is. Then `/regen` asks again with
  the shorter conversation.

Measured at a window of 4096: a page pasted after a first turn of 3446 tokens
was cut by Ollama from 5210 tokens to 2051, and the feed said *of at least 4357
tokens it processed 2051*. At 16384, and at 4096 without the paste, nothing was
told, because nothing was cut.

Only an external server is judged. llama-server and the cloud providers refuse a
prompt that does not fit, and mindfork already says what to do then.

## Fixed

- **The window-too-small note described Ollama's cut wrongly.** It said Ollama
  cuts from the start, the instructions first. That happens only when the last
  message alone does not fit; otherwise the oldest messages go. The note and the
  [install guide](@/install.md) say both now.

## Your data

Nothing in the data's format changes, and no step runs at the start.
